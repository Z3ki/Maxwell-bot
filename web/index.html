Getting started

No dashboard needed.

If Maxwell is already available in your Discord, start with a question. New to the project? The guide also covers running your own bot.

01 / ASK
/maxwell

Write your request. Replies are public by default; choose visibility: Private for a reply only you can see.

02 / MAKE IT YOURS

A few things to know

Before you ask.

Can I turn public replies off?

Yes. Choose Private in the command's visibility option, or open /config → Default visibility → Private to save it for future requests. Private replies still pass through Maxwell and its configured AI providers.

What can server admins change?

Members with Manage Server can choose response channels, available tools, moderation, and progress updates. Personal reply preferences apply to your own requests.

Is Maxwell ready for anything?

It's still in public alpha. Features and limits can change, and outages or data loss can happen. Check important answers and keep a copy of files you need.

What happens to my messages?

Maxwell processes the context needed for your request and may store conversation memory. Relevant content goes to configured AI and tool providers. Read the Privacy Policy for details and deletion options.
