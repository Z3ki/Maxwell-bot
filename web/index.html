Getting started

Four commands
cover most of it.

If Maxwell is in your server, or you've added it to your account, start with a question. Everything else can wait until you need it.

01 / ASK/maxwell

Write your request. Replies are public by default; pick visibility: Private for one only you can see.

02 / RIGHT-CLICKApps →

On any message, open Apps and pick Ask Maxwell, Summarize, Explain, Fact-check, or Rewrite / Translate.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS/config

Language, answer length, visibility, personality, and your AI provider, right inside Discord.

04 / SIGN IN/dashboard

A private link to the web dashboard for settings, files, schedules, and servers.

Also handy: /cancel stops your running request, /tasks shows your subagent jobs, and /music controls playback. Read the full guide ↗

Good to know

Before you ask.

Can I turn public replies off?

Yes. Pick Private in the command's visibility option, or save Private as your default in /config or the dashboard. Private replies still pass through Maxwell and its configured AI providers.

What happens to files when the sandbox resets?

The code sandbox is wiped after about ten idle minutes. Premium /storage is saved before the wipe and restored on your next command, and subagent results are copied there when a job finishes. Anything outside /storage is temporary.

Who can see my public folder?

Anyone with the link. Files under your public folder are served at /u/<your handle>/. The rest of your storage stays private; share a single file with a signed link that expires.

How do schedules work?

A schedule is a saved prompt plus a time: a cron pattern such as weekdays at 9, or a single date. When it's due, Maxwell runs the prompt in the background and posts the result where you made the schedule, or in your DMs if you prefer. Runs are at least 15 minutes apart, and Maxwell can create schedules for you when you ask.

What can server admins change?

Members with Manage Server choose response channels, available tools, moderation, and progress messages, in /config or the dashboard's Servers page. Personal reply preferences apply only to your own requests.

Is Maxwell ready for anything?

It's in public alpha. Features and limits can change, and outages or data loss can happen. Check important answers and keep copies of files you care about.

What happens to my messages?

Maxwell processes the context needed for your request and may keep conversation memory. Relevant content goes to the configured AI and tool providers. The Privacy Policy covers details and how to delete your data.
