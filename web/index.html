Is Maxwell free?

Yes. The hosted bot is free to use with an hourly message allowance, and the code is MIT licensed. Premium is a manual grant from the operator. There are no paid plans.

Should I add it to a server or to my account?

Add it to a server to give everyone there slash commands and right-click apps. Add it to your account to use it in DMs, group chats, and servers that allow external apps. You can do both.

Can other people see my replies?

Replies are public by default. Pick visibility: Private on a request, or save Private as your default in /config, and only you will see them.

What happens to my files when the sandbox resets?

The sandbox is wiped after about ten idle minutes. Hosted sites and games stay live. On Premium, everything in /storage is saved and restored, and finished subagent builds are copied to /storage/jobs/.

How do schedules work?

A schedule is a saved prompt plus a time. When it's due, Maxwell runs the prompt as a background job and posts the result where you made it, or in your DMs. Ask in plain words or set one up in the dashboard.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. In /config → My AI provider or the dashboard, pick a provider and model, save your key and test it. Keys are stored encrypted and you can delete them at any time.

Can I run my own Maxwell?

Yes. You need Docker, a Discord bot token and a compatible model endpoint, and the installer handles the rest. See Run your own Maxwell.
