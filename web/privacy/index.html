Privacy Policy

Saved conversation history is optional. Turning it off does not stop requests from being processed by AI providers.

Last updated 17 October 2026 · Policy version 2026-10-17

Deployment review pending. These pages describe this release candidate. The operator must verify its identity, private support, provider configuration, retention and applicable legal decisions before public release. This is not a compliance certification.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of the Maxwell instance you use controls its configuration and data. This release candidate requires operator identity and private support verification before public distribution. Self-hosted operators must configure their own policy URLs; Maxwell does not substitute someone else’s policies.

2. What is processed and why

Maxwell processes Discord user, server, channel and message identifiers; names and permissions needed for the request; the current message, selected reply/context and relevant attachments; tool arguments/results needed to perform requested actions; and limited usage/security metadata. Optional preferences, account links, published files, sites, reminders and tool workspaces are retained when you request those features. Do not submit prohibited sensitive information.

Processing serves the requested functionality, optional preferences, authentication, abuse prevention and operational reliability. The operator must assess applicable lawful bases, local privacy rights and required transfer safeguards. This page does not assert GDPR, CCPA or other legal compliance or treat all processing as consent-based.

3. Optional conversation history and memory

For new accounts, saved history is off. Historical default-on values and discontinued consent records are not treated as affirmative agreement; unverified historical conversation data is removed during cleanup. An explicit current On choice saves an optional-storage record; existing opt-outs remain off. Reset never turns storage on.

With history off, current requests and explicitly selected essential context can still be processed, but conversation content, linked tool transcript rows and replies are not added to saved history. Automatic learned facts and embeddings are retired; legacy facts/vectors are removed by cleanup. Personalities and other user-entered preferences are separate optional settings. Explicitly requested published output, reminders, account integrations and workspaces are separate feature records, not hidden conversation history.

4. External processing and transfers

Primary/fallback chat and vision providers, BYOK providers, search services and image/audio providers receive the content needed for their part of a requested feature. The configured provider may change on fallback. Those services and their subprocessors may operate in other countries. Local-history Off is not a provider opt-out or a guarantee of zero retention, no training, a particular region or provider-side deletion. The operator must verify and disclose the actual active providers, account tiers, routing and terms before release.

Examples of provider documentation: Mistral retention controls and Gemini API terms. These examples do not establish this instance’s configuration. Maxwell does not train AI models on Discord message content.

5. Voice and account connections

Reference speech requires your explicit authorization for the exact reference. Only a hash identifying the reference and authorization metadata are saved, not a voice sample in the consent store. Temporary audio is processed to synthesize the requested line; Discord and the speech provider may keep their own copies. Withdraw optional authorizations in Privacy. Account linking, including Last.fm and GitHub, uses its own user-initiated authorization; a history choice does not authorize unrelated integrations.

Premium task workers use an independent worker connection when configured, otherwise personal chat BYOK, then the operator worker model or primary provider. Optional worker credentials and their model/settings are encrypted separately from chat credentials, retained until removal or full deletion. Workers use the selected AI provider and the requester’s existing tool permissions. Jobs continue while chat remains available. Task text and transcripts stay in memory while working; final reports are cached in bounded memory for up to one hour and disappear on restart or full deletion. A separate job database retains only user/job/source/channel/guild IDs, visibility, kinds, timestamps, state, side-effect/delivery markers and tool counts for one day by default (operator configurable). It stores no task text, transcripts, reports, webhook tokens or API keys. Restart marks unfinished work interrupted and never replays it automatically. Use /tasks for private review/cancel and to retrieve an available result if the original interaction expired. Completion respects the original visibility; private results are never moved to public channels or unsolicited DMs. Generated websites and shell files follow those features’ separate lifecycles. Explicitly saved custom agent profiles hold an ID, instructions and tool permissions separately from history, up to ten per user, and expire 90 days after editing by default or on full deletion. They contain no separate API keys. Manual plan grants store only the user ID and plan/configuration until revocation or personal-data deletion; granting Premium does not take a payment.

6. Retention defaults

Cleanup runs at startup and about every ten minutes. Saved transcript rows have a default maximum age of 30 days and a per-channel count bound; inactive channel history is also cleared after 12 hours. Diagnostics stored by this candidate contain identifiers, status and tool names rather than raw messages, arguments or model reasoning; legacy reasoning traces are scrubbed. Trace and usage/request metadata expire after seven days by default. Optional withdrawn consent records expire after 365 days; active choices remain until withdrawal or deletion. A notice receipt holds only user ID, version and time to avoid repeated notices.

Dashboard login flows expire after five minutes, sessions after at most eight hours. Expired session/flow rows are pruned on authentication activity and periodic cleanup. BYOK credentials remain until removal; optional preferences remain until reset/deletion. Reminders and callback actions follow their feature lifetime. Published sites/files, running workspaces, operator logs and backups require the operator’s configured lifecycle and may need manual removal. There is no verified backup deadline for this candidate; the operator must establish one before release. Retention defaults are configurable and must be disclosed when changed.

7. Deletion and its limits

Use /config → Privacy → Off to stop and delete saved conversation content. Delete my data in Privacy or the dashboard also processes supported personal preferences, optional consents, custom agent profiles, worker job metadata and cached reports, manual Premium grants, BYOK credentials, reminders, callback records, traces, GitHub account metadata and dashboard sessions where available. A small storage-suppression record remains so old writes cannot recreate erased history. Short-lived anti-abuse usage metadata may remain.

Deletion reports failed subsystems and can be retried. The result does not claim all copies are gone. Published files/sites, shell/GitHub workspaces, inbox/thread and optional-plugin records may require stopping active jobs and operator intervention. Backups, operator support messages, Discord posts and existing third-party copies cannot be erased by this button. The operator must apply deletion suppression after restoring backups and handle provider/backup requests through private support.

8. Security, cookies and credentials

BYOK credentials and dashboard session payloads use authenticated encryption with an operator-managed key. Session cookies are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure for HTTPS deployments; settings changes require a CSRF token. Discord OAuth requests identify/guild permissions to manage your account and authorized servers. This does not authorize unrelated processing. The repository does not provide encrypted storage for every SQLite/JSON file: disk/database encryption, access control, backup protection, HTTPS and incident response remain deployment requirements. No security certification is claimed.

9. Your choices and support

Review, withdraw and delete optional choices in /config → Privacy. Request private support there for access, export, correction, workspace/backup deletion or other applicable rights. The support form sends only what you choose to the configured operator; do not include credentials. Public issue trackers are unsuitable for personal requests. Applicable rights and retention exceptions depend on law and must be assessed by the operator.

Policies are versioned. Delivering a disclosure never records agreement; server administrators cannot supply each member’s optional consent. No unsolicited legal DMs or public consent prompts are sent.
