Deployment review pending. These pages describe this release candidate. The operator must verify its identity, private support, provider configuration, retention and applicable legal decisions before public release. This is not a compliance certification.

1. Using Maxwell

Maxwell is an AI application on Discord. Use it only if you meet Discord’s minimum age of 13 or the higher local minimum and any age requirements of the selected provider. If you need a parent or guardian’s agreement under applicable law, obtain it before using the service. These terms do not replace Discord’s terms.

Reading a notice is not consent to every kind of processing. Maxwell does not record agreement from passive channel presence, server installation, a policy link, or a server administrator’s action on someone else’s behalf. Optional saved history and voice authorizations have separate controls.

2. Operator and scope

The responsible operator is the person or entity running the bot you are using; the open-source project does not operate every copy. The operator must identify itself and establish private support before public distribution. For this instance, use /config → Privacy → Request private support. Public GitHub issues are not a private support channel.

3. Service and permitted use

Features depend on configuration. Follow applicable law, Discord’s Terms and Community Guidelines, provider rules, and other people’s rights. Do not use Maxwell for harassment, fraud, child sexual abuse material, unauthorized voice cloning, privacy invasion, credential theft, spam or unauthorized access. Do not submit prohibited sensitive data. The operator may restrict access to protect users, investigate abuse, comply with law or shut down the service.

4. AI output and availability

AI output can be inaccurate, biased, incomplete or invented. Verify important information; it is not professional advice or an emergency service. Availability, model selection and quotas may change. Keep your own copies of important work.

5. Your content and third parties

You retain your rights in submitted content. Provide only content you have the right to share and authorize processing needed for the feature you request. Requests, selected context and relevant media are transmitted to the configured primary, fallback, vision, search or media providers, including a BYOK provider when selected. Saved conversation history is on by default and can be turned off in /config → Privacy. Provider processing and retention are separate from local storage; see Privacy.

6. Audio and voice

Preset speech is available without reference-cloning authorization. To clone a reference, you must affirm that you own the voice or have the speaker’s explicit permission for that reference in the private Privacy controls. Public availability, a recording URL or another person’s upload alone is not permission. Do not impersonate deceptively or violate anyone’s rights. Audio and text needed to synthesize speech go to the configured speech provider.

7. Premium and billing

Manual Premium grants may provide higher message allowances, a configured model, bounded task workers (subagents), persistent storage up to the configured quota, a public folder, schedules and longer shell commands. Content you publish from a public folder must follow these terms, and the operator may remove it or turn publishing off. Grants do not take payment, bypass tool permissions or guarantee completion. This candidate does not establish new paid subscription terms. Before enabling sales, the operator must publish the actual seller, price, billing interval, benefits, renewal, cancellation, refund and tax information, and follow Discord’s applicable monetization requirements. No future feature or unlimited availability is promised.

8. Suspension, liability and applicable law

You may stop using Maxwell and request deletion at any time. The service is provided as available, without promises of uninterrupted or error-free operation. Any disclaimer or limitation applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law and does not remove mandatory consumer rights. These terms do not select a governing jurisdiction or require arbitration; mandatory applicable law remains in effect. The operator must review the contract design and any chosen jurisdiction before a public release.

9. Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be identified with a version/date. Maxwell does not request acceptance after every deployment or treat continued passive presence as fresh optional consent. Where a feature requires a new affirmative choice, only that feature is gated. Use /help, /config → Privacy, the dashboard or the Contact page to review policies and obtain support.
