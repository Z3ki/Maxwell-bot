5. What the service does

Maxwell is an official Discord bot with chat, memory, and tools. Depending on configuration it may read messages directed at it, remember things, search the web, generate images, publish static sites, run sandboxed shell jobs, and process supported attachments. Maxwell does not join voice channels or listen to calls. AI output is often wrong. You are responsible for what you do with it.

Reply visibility. /maxwell replies are public by default. You can choose Private for one request or save it in /config. Public replies are visible to people with access to that channel. Private replies are visible only to the requester in Discord, but are still processed by Maxwell and its configured providers.
